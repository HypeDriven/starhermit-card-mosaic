    How to play

    The goal

    Fill every cell of the mosaic. Wherever two cards touch, the motifs on the touching edges must match.

    Placing cards

    Select a card in the tray, then choose an empty cell. Keyboard: arrows move, Enter confirms.

    Matching edges

    Each matched edge glows and scores 10 points. Mismatched edges are tinted — they never block you, but they score nothing.

    Rotating

    Some cards arrive turned. Select a tray card and Rotate (R) to quarter-turn it before placing.

    Locking

    A card that matches every placed neighbor can be locked (L) for 15 points. Locked cards cannot move.

    Swapping & recall

    Tap two placed cards to swap them (−5 points). Recall returns a placed card to its tray slot.

    Invalid moves

    Illegal tries are always reversible and cost 2 points — the game explains why each one failed.

    Impostors

    Some trays hold decoy cards that belong nowhere. Leave them aside to finish the true mosaic.
